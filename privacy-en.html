OfflineBible Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the English edition of OfflineBible for iPhone and iPad.

Data stored on your device

You can read and search the Bible without creating an account or signing in. The app stores settings, reading positions and history, bookmarks, highlights, memos, and verse notes on your device. The app does not send this content to the developer's servers.

When you back up your app data, the app saves a file to the location you choose. If you choose a cloud storage service, its privacy policy applies. You can restore your data by selecting the backup file in the app.

Advertising and consent

OfflineBible uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad impressions, app or ad interactions, and crash and performance data. Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics, and diagnose SDK issues.

The app disables ad personalization. When ad consent is required, the app displays Google's consent form. If ad privacy options are available, you can change your choices in the app's settings.

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for iOS

In-app purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores for ad removal through the App Store. The app checks the purchase status Apple provides to apply ad removal. The developer does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password.

Retention, deletion, and contact

You can delete memos and verse notes within the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Backup files saved separately remain in the location you chose until you delete them there.

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete personal information, email the developer. Your email address and message are used to respond to your inquiry.
