Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the app offline?

Bible reading and search work without an internet connection. Advertisements and in-app purchases require an internet connection.

How do I change the text size or theme?

Open Settings to adjust text size, line spacing, and the light or dark theme.

How do I remove advertisements?

Open Settings and choose Remove Ads. If you previously purchased ad removal with your Apple Account, choose Restore Purchases.

How do I keep my saved data when changing devices?

Use Back Up Data in Settings and keep the backup file in a safe location. On your new device, use Restore Data and select that file.
